- Count-rule vs render-rule are near-identical but not byte-identical: REJECTED_SQL (approvals.ts:106) treats any decision object with action:"rejected" as rejected regardless of the rest of the shape, while DecidedRow.decisionOf (DecidedRow.tsx:22) requires a full valid record (by.name, at) and otherwise falls back to legacy !== "rejected" -> renders approved.
