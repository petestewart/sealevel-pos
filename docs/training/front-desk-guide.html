For the trainer

Running the session

Walk through the sections in order with an iPad on the counter, one person driving. The flow that matters most is sections 4 to 6: check-in, the two red and amber rows, and a walk-in. Selling (9 to 12) can be faster if your group already sells in Mindbody.

  1. Make sure everyone knows their own Mindbody username and password before the session. Section 2 does not work without it.
  2. Have each teacher sign in once and set their PIN during the session, so nobody meets that screen for the first time with a queue.
  3. Practise on a test student, not a real one. If a banner across the top of the iPad says Dry run or SANDBOX, taps are practice and do not touch real students (see section 15).

The people in the screenshots are made up. Every screen is the real app.

Section 1

Before you start

The front desk app is a faster way to do the things you already do in Mindbody before class. It reads and writes straight to Mindbody, so anything you do here shows up in Mindbody right away, under your name.

The iPad has four buttons along the bottom:

  • Sign-in: the class list. This is where you spend most of your time.
  • Cart: the shelf of passes, memberships, drinks, clothing, rentals and gift cards.
  • Pay: taking payment for what is in the cart.
  • Your name: who is signed in, your PIN, and Sign out.
Three habits
  • Wait for the answer. When you check someone in, the button spins while Mindbody answers (usually under a second). It only turns green when the student is really checked in.
  • Sign in as yourself. Everything you do is recorded under your Mindbody login. Never use someone else's.
  • Keep your PIN to yourself. It is your signature for discounts, comps and overrides.
Section 2

Signing in

The first thing the iPad shows is a sign-in box. Every teacher signs in with their own Mindbody login.

Sign in with your own Mindbody login

  1. Type your Mindbody username (your email) and password. These are the same ones you use for the Mindbody app or website.
  2. Tap Sign in.
Good to know

A sign-in lasts two hours, then this screen comes back and you sign in again. That is normal. If the studio account or an owner login is refused, use your own teacher login instead.

Set up your PIN (first time only)

The first time you sign in, the app asks you to choose a personal PIN of 4 to 6 digits. You will type it whenever you give a discount or comp, check someone in without a waiver, or override something. Your name is recorded each time.

  1. Type a PIN you will remember and nobody can guess, then tap Next.
  2. Type it again. It saves as soon as the second entry matches, and a PIN set bar appears at the top of the screen.
Careful

Not now skips this, but you will be stuck the first time a student needs a discount or a waiver override. Set it up now. If two teachers pick the same PIN the app asks the second one to choose another.

Section 3

The Sign-in screen

After you sign in you land on the class that is about to start (or the one that started in the last two hours). Each row is one student booked into that class.

  1. Change day. A calendar for looking at another day's classes.
  2. The class on screen. Date, time, class and teacher. Tap it to switch to another class today.
  3. Refresh. Reloads the list. It also refreshes by itself every 30 seconds, so people who check in on the Mindbody app appear on their own.
  4. Counters. How many are signed up, checked in and on the waiting list. Tap Checked in or Waitlist to see the names.
  5. Search for a walk-in. Find anyone in Mindbody to add them to this class (section 6).
  6. New client. Create a Mindbody account for someone who has never been here.
  7. Line number and name. Under the name, how often they have been coming ("3rd class this week", "Last here Sep 4"). A brand-new student has nothing there.
  8. M, the star and the info icon. M means a member: tap it for their membership. ★ means this is their first class at the studio (5.5). The ⓘ icon opens alerts and notes; it is dark when there is something to read and faint when there is nothing.
  9. Payment. The pass paying for this class, how many classes are left and when it expires. 1 remaining is highlighted: this is their last class on the pass. No pass in amber means the booking is not paid.
  10. Account. Their account balance. Red with a minus sign means they owe the studio money.
  11. The check-in button. Tap it to check the student in. Its colour tells you the state (table below).
  12. Row tools. Trash can: remove from class. $: open the Cart for this student. Person: their profile. Arrow: open them in Mindbody.
  13. The four bottom buttons: Sign-in, Cart, Pay, and your name.

Good to know

On a future day you can book people but not check them in; a banner says "Booking only; check-in opens on the day." On a past day a banner warns that every change is recorded with your name.

Section 4

Checking students in

For most students this is one tap.

  1. Find their row. If the list is long, the arrows icon at the right end of the column headings sorts it by first or last name.
  2. Tap check in. It turns into a spinner and the line under their name says Talking to Mindbody...
  3. When it turns green, ✓ checked in, they are done. The Checked in counter goes up by one.

Fixing a mistake

Checked in the wrong person? Tap their green ✓ checked in button and confirm Check out. They are marked as not attended and stay booked.

Someone is not coming, or was booked into the wrong class? Tap the trash can on their row and confirm Remove. This cancels their booking. The trash can only appears on rows that are not checked in, so check them out first if needed.

Last class on a pass

When a check-in uses the last class on someone's pass, the app offers to sell them the next one right there. It suggests the same pass and shows which card or balance will pay.

  • If they want it, pick a pass and tap Charge $115.00 (the amount changes with the pass).
  • If not, tap Not now. They are still checked in.
Section 5

Rows that need attention

Red and amber buttons mean the app needs something from you before it can check the student in.

5.1 No waiver

Every student has to agree to the studio's liability waiver once. If they have not, the row shows no waiver.

  1. Tap no waiver, then Read the waiver.
  2. Hand the iPad to the student. They read the waiver to the end (scrolling down if it is long).
  3. They tap Record agreement and check in. Their agreement is saved in Mindbody and they are checked in.

When they cannot sign right now

If the waiver will not load, or there is a good reason to let them in without signing today, you can override with your PIN. You must type a reason.

  1. Tap Check in without a waiver (it is at the bottom of the waiver box).
  2. Type the reason, for example "Signing on the Mindbody app after class, phone was dead".
  3. Type your PIN and tap Done.
Careful

An override does not sign the waiver. The same red button will be there next visit until they actually agree to it. Use it as an exception, not a shortcut.

5.2 Unpaid

unpaid means the student booked but nothing is paying for the class. Tapping it opens Pay and check in.

  1. Pick what they are buying. Drop In is selected to start with.
  2. Check the line under the list. It says what will pay, for example Pays with the stored card ...8210.
  3. Tap Charge $25.00 and check in. The button steps through charging, attaching the pass and checking in.
  • Paying cash, or no card on file? The button says Cart and check in instead. It takes you to the Cart with the pass already on the ticket; take payment there and the app checks them in afterwards. See section 9.
  • Check in free (comp) lets them in without paying. Only use it when the studio has said that class is free for them.

5.3 Alerts and notes

Tap the ⓘ icon on a row to read what the studio has written about a student. A red alert is something you must act on (shown in red); a yellow alert is something to know (an injury, a preference); notes are general.

Tap the pencil next to any of them to edit. What you save is signed with your name and date.

5.4 Members and balances

Tap the M badge to see a member's contract and passes. A red amount in the Account column (like -$12.50) means they owe the studio; let them know, or settle it in the Cart.

5.5 First class

A gold ★ next to the name means the class on screen is the student's first class at the studio, going by Mindbody's records. Take a moment with them: show them the room, the water and the towels. A first-timer usually also needs to agree to the waiver.

Good to know

Only their very first class gets the star. If a new student books two classes on their first day, only the earlier one has it. A walk-in you add from search who has never been here gets it too.

Section 6

Walk-ins and new students

Anyone who is not on the list: search for them first, and only create a new account if they are really not in Mindbody.

Find them and add them to the class

  1. Type at least three letters of their first name, last name or email into Search for a walk-in. Results appear as you type.
  2. Check it is the right person (the email and phone are under the name), then tap their row. They are added to the class on screen.
  3. Find them at the bottom of the list and check them in. If they have no pass, the row is unpaid; tap it to sell them one (5.2).
Good to know

If the person has never signed the waiver, the waiver box opens as soon as you tap them, before they are added. Hand them the iPad as in 5.1.

A brand-new student

  1. Tap New client (top right of the Sign-in screen, or in the search results when nobody is found).
  2. Fill in first name, last name, email and phone. Tick the email boxes if they want account emails or news and offers.
  3. Tap Create. They now have a Mindbody account and show up in the results, ready to tap into the class.
  4. They will be asked to agree to the waiver when you add them to the class. Hand them the iPad.
Careful

If Mindbody says "already has a client with this name and email", they already have an account. Close the form and search for them instead.

Section 7

Guests on a member's pass

Some memberships come with guest passes. When a member has one left, their row shows a small person with a plus icon next to their pass.

  1. Tap the guest icon on the member's row.
  2. Search for the guest and press Enter, or pick them from In class if they already booked. If they are new, tap New client.
  3. Read the sentence that explains what will happen, then tap Confirm. The guest is checked in on the member's guest pass.
Section 8

Full classes and the waiting list

When a class is full, the search box says so, and adding someone puts them on the waiting list instead.

  1. Search and tap the person as usual. A box asks This class is full.
  2. Tap Add to waiting list.
  3. The waiting list appears at the bottom of the class. When a spot opens, tap promote on their row to move them into the class.
Section 9

Selling

Selling has two steps: Cart (what they are buying) and Pay (how they are paying).

Open the Cart

  • For a student in the class: tap the $ icon on their row. The cart opens with their name at the top (Sale for Jordan Kim), so their card on file and account balance are ready to use.
  • For anyone else: tap Cart at the bottom. Then tap Attach a client to search for them, Walk-in for a quick sale to someone you do not need to name (a bottle of water), or New client.

Build the ticket

  1. Pick a section on the left: Passes, Retail (Food/Drink, Clothing, Accessories, Gift cards) or Rentals. Starred items appear under Favorites.
  2. Tap an item to add it to the Ticket on the right. Use − and + on the item to change the quantity, or tap a line on the ticket to change or remove it.
  3. Check the total, then tap Pay.
Good to know

A pass always belongs to a person, so a walk-in sale cannot include one. The app will ask who the pass is for. To buy a pass for someone else (a parent paying for a teen), tap the ticket line and choose Other Client.

Take payment

  1. On the Pay screen, tap how they are paying: Account, Card (the card on file), Cash or Gift card.
  2. Leave Email receipt ticked if they want one.
  3. Tap Finalize Sale. The card is charged at this moment.
  4. Read the green Sale complete box, then tap Done to go back to the class.

Cash

  1. Tap Cash. It fills in the exact amount.
  2. If they hand you more, tap the amount and enter what they gave you, or tap a quick button: Exact $5 $10 $20. The keypad shows the change due. Tap Done.
  3. Tap Finalize Sale. The receipt box tells you how much change to give from the drawer.

Other ways to pay

  • A card that is not on file: tap the small keypad square on the Card tile and type the card in. You can use it once, or keep it on file for next time.
  • Account: uses their account credit. Charging more than they have puts their account below zero and needs your PIN.
  • Gift card: type or scan the gift card number and tap Check balance.
  • Splitting: a sale can be paid two ways (some cash, the rest on card). Tap a second tile and adjust the amounts.
  • Card sales under $10: the card is charged $10 and the difference stays on their account as credit for next time. Tell the student.
Section 10

Discounts and comps

A discount takes money off a sale. A comp makes the whole sale free. Both need a reason and your PIN, and both are recorded with your name.

  1. On the Pay screen, tap Discount.
  2. Choose Percent, Amount, or Entire sale (a comp). Tap a quick button like 20% or type the number on the keypad. The line underneath says what they will pay.
  3. Choose a reason: Trade, Damaged item or Other. Trade and Other need a short note. Tap Next.
  4. Type your PIN and tap Done.
  5. Check the summary and tap Discount (or Comp). Then finish the sale as usual.
Section 11

Memberships

A membership is an ongoing monthly contract charged to the student's card on file. It is sold from its own box, not the ticket.

  1. Open the Cart for the student (they must have a card on file; if not, the box offers Add card).
  2. Under Passes → Memberships, tap the membership.
  3. Go through the box with the student: the first payment today, the monthly amount, the contract total, and the terms. Tap Starts to pick a later start date if they want one.
  4. Tap the long button that describes the charge, for example Charge $149.00 today, then $149.00 monthly...
  5. The app asks for the student's signature on the customer screen, which we are not using yet. Tap Sell without a signature, type your PIN, and tap Done.
Careful

Your PIN here records that you sold the membership without a signature on the iPad. Make sure the student has heard the terms and agrees before you type it.

Section 12

Gift cards

Gift cards are under Retail → Gift cards. The app makes up the card's number; you write it on a blank card.

  1. Tap a gift card, or Custom for any amount (typed on the keypad).
  2. Take payment as usual and tap Finalize Sale.
  3. The receipt box shows the gift card number in large letters. Write it on the blank gift card, then tap Written on the card. The app will not let you leave until you do.
Section 13

Student profile and card on file

Tap the person icon on any row to see a student's phone, email, waiver, visits, passes and card on file.

To add or update a card, tap Add card or Replace card and type it in (or let the student do it). Mindbody keeps the card; the iPad never stores it.

Email preferences can be ticked or unticked here and save by themselves. Text message preferences are set in Mindbody.

Section 14

Signing out

When you hand the desk to another teacher, sign out so their work is recorded under their name, not yours.

  1. Tap your name in the bottom right.
  2. Tap Sign out. The next teacher signs in with their own login.
Section 15

When something goes wrong

The app always says what happened in a sentence. These are the ones you are most likely to meet.

You seeWhat it means and what to do
The sign-in screen comes backYour two hours are up, or the iPad was restarted. Sign in again. If it says "Nothing was written", repeat what you were doing.
Mindbody did not accept that sign-in.Wrong username or password. Try the Mindbody app with the same login to check it.
That is not your PIN. / That PIN does not match any teacher.Your PIN was mistyped. Try again. If you have forgotten it, sign in again and use Change PIN (section 14).
A red line under a nameMindbody refused that check-in or booking and the sentence says why. Fix it (a pass, the waiver) and tap again, or do it in Mindbody.
Done as the studio account: ...It worked, but your Mindbody login is missing a permission, so it was recorded under the studio's account. Tell the manager.
Not charged: ...Nothing was charged. It is safe to try again or use another way to pay.
The charge may or may not have gone through.Do not charge again. Check the student's account in Mindbody first, or ask the manager.
Totals disagree. Do not charge.The price on screen and Mindbody's price differ. Tap Recheck prices. If it stays, ring it up in Mindbody and tell the manager.
A banner across the top saying Dry run or SANDBOXPractice mode. Taps do nothing in the real Mindbody. On a normal day there is no banner at all. If you see one before real classes, tell the manager.
Your sign-in belongs to a different Mindbody site.Sign in again. If it keeps happening, tell the manager.
First-class stars on regularsThe star is wrong for that class. Ignore it and tell the manager which class and student.
When in doubt

Anything the iPad cannot do, you can still do in Mindbody exactly as before. The arrow icon on each row opens that student in Mindbody.

Section 16

Quick reference

Start of shift

  • Your Mindbody email and password, Sign in
  • Sign in again every two hours

Check in

  • Tap check in, wait for green
  • ★ = their first class: welcome them
  • Wrong person: tap green, Check out
  • Not coming: trash can, Remove

Red and amber rows

  • no waiver: hand them the iPad to read and agree
  • Can't sign: Check in without a waiver, reason, PIN
  • unpaid: pick a pass, Charge and check in

Walk-ins

  • Search 3+ letters, tap the person
  • Not in Mindbody: New client, then add
  • Full class: Add to waiting list

Selling

  • $ on the row, or Cart
  • Tap items, then Pay
  • Choose Card, Cash, Account or Gift card
  • Finalize Sale, then Done

  • Discounts and comps
