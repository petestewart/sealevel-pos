Buy screen layout mockups

Static visuals for docs/design/sale-screen-layout.md, revised after Pete's review: vertical category rail on the left, item grid in the middle, cart on the right, and a density pass over every padding, gap and label. Nothing here is wired up. Every frame paints the app's own tokens explicitly, in both palettes. Items and prices are the studio's real ones at the in-studio rate, before the 10.35% tax the cart adds. No photos, by decision: the type and the layout carry it.

1. Buy, shelf mode. Light palette, 1400px shell. Pete's three columns: rail left, grid middle, cart right. A realistic seven-line cart, nine items, and none of it scrolls. Liquid IV is the selected cart line, so it is the only one showing quantity controls; the single-quantity lines are one line of type each, because "1 @ $2.72" only repeats the total beside it. Cards already in the cart carry their count inline after the price. No Comp control anywhere on this screen.
